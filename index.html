Our Community Code of Care

The values that guide everything we build

  • Consent first: You choose what to share, when to share it, and with whom. Your information belongs to you.
  • Lived experience matters: We believe you are the expert on your own body, needs, and experiences.
  • No hierarchy of needs: All support needs are valid. We don't compare or rank what people need to thrive.
